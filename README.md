### How I think

- **Problem solving:** Breaking complex problems into smaller,
  manageable pieces.
- **Algorithms:** Exploring different approaches, edge cases,
  time complexity and space complexity.
- **Engineering:** Understanding why something works,
  not just making it work.
- **Experimentation:** Trying unfamiliar technologies,
  questioning assumptions and learning from failures.
- **Entrepreneurship:** Connecting technical possibilities
  with practical human and business needs.

I enjoy working through problems using data structures,
